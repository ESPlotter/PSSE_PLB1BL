# Code of Conduct

ESPlotter welcomes respectful participation from people with different backgrounds, experience levels, and viewpoints.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, constructive, and respectful in discussions and reviews.
- Critique ideas and code without attacking people.
- Keep feedback focused on improving the project and helping others participate.
- Respect maintainers' decisions and the time of other contributors.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discriminatory language, insults, threats, or personal attacks.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Deliberately disrupting discussions, reviews, or project work.
- Any other conduct that a reasonable person would consider inappropriate in a professional community.

## Scope and enforcement

This code applies to project spaces, including issues, pull requests, and other channels used to represent or discuss the project.

Report a concern to [support@esplotter.com](mailto:support@esplotter.com). Project maintainers will review reports, may remove content, and may restrict participation when needed to keep the community safe and constructive. Please include links and relevant context; do not post private reports as public issues.
